Palisade runs an AI assistant entirely on this Mac. A guardian model checks every message and every answer against a written policy, and each exchange is recorded in an audit log that shows tampering.

On the Installation Type step you choose how it runs:

  • Service mode: a locked-down background account runs Palisade and writes the audit log. Your administrator account can review the log but not change it.
  • Desktop mode: Palisade runs under your own login, with your own settings and data.

The installer is small. The first time Palisade starts, it downloads two models (about 10 GB) and checks each one against the fingerprint published with this release.
