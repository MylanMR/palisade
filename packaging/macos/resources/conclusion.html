Palisade is installed.

Open Palisade from your Applications folder. The first start downloads the models (about 10 GB); the page shows progress, and Palisade starts on its own when the download is verified.

In service mode, log out and back in once so your account can read the audit log. Then see "Audit log procedures" in the README for reviewing it.

To remove Palisade later: sudo /opt/palisade/uninstall.sh. Your audit log is kept.
